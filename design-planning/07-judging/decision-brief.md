## 1. Executive summary

**Recommended (not chosen — the human decides): Finalist A — "Instrument."** It tops the weighted score
(**2.98 / 4**), and it wins the two highest-weighted central-problem bets — **D4 one-product coherence
(=4, the literal one-component board↔roadmap toggle)** and **D5 dual-audience (=4)** — which is exactly
where the rubric concentrates weight (D4=14, the heaviest dimension). **Finalist D — "Civic" is the
runner-up (2.89)**, within one rounding-step, on the strength of owning **D9 sovereignty (=4)** outright.

**Headline: all four finalists PASSED both hard gates (G1 accessibility, G2 i18n/RTL) at the expert-audit
floor.** No finalist is non-conformant; no hard Blocker was found in any of them. **So this is a
taste/strategy choice among four conformant directions, not a conformance cull.** The spread was designed
in (6b): A=one-skin power, B=warm dual-audience, C=foregrounded-agent, D=always-on sovereignty — four real,
non-overlapping bets. The scores are *close at the top* (A 2.98 / D 2.89 / B 2.82) and the right reading is
"pick the strategy," not "pick the winner." A synthesized hybrid (§6) is the panel's likely best end-state.

## 4. Recommendation + reasoning — **recommended, not chosen — the human decides**

**Recommend Finalist A — "Instrument."**

- **It wins where the rubric puts its weight.** The brief names **D4 one-product coherence** as *the central
  problem* and gives it the **heaviest weight (14)**. A scores it **4** — the only finalist with a literal
  one-component proof (the single-toggle board↔roadmap density-tune over the same ISS-377 data, shared atoms,
  no per-screen redefinition). It also owns **D5 dual-audience = 4** (the in-place lens switch, neither lens
  starved). Coherence + dual-audience are Myelin's defining "five-surfaces-as-one" bet, and A is the
  reference answer to it.
- **It is the only finalist where the power-user spine is actually built.** PROVEN: 3 wired `keydown`
  handlers + a working lens toggle (vs D=1, B=0, C=0). For a product whose wedge is keyboard-first speed
  (D1=12), that matters.
- **The lovability mandate is the live tension, and A is the *least* lovable-at-first-glance** (D2=2,
  cold command-deck). The rubric's "reason-to-love" weight is carried mostly by D3 (B wins, 3.5) and D2 (B
  wins, 4). **A wins on coherence + power; B wins on warmth.** The recommendation favours A because the
  *central problem* (D4) and the highest aggregate score point there — **but the warmth gap is the single
  biggest reason a human might overrule toward B**, and the hybrid (§6) is designed to close it.

This is **recommended, not chosen.** The margins are small and the bets are real; §5 makes the case against,
and §7 guarantees that picking otherwise is a re-run, not a rebuild.

## 5. The dissenting case for the runner-up (and for C / D as strategy picks)

**Honest case for D — "Civic" (runner-up, 2.89 — 0.09 behind):**
- **If sovereignty-first is the strategy, D should win outright.** Myelin's stated market is **EU public-sector
  procurement** (EN 301 549 / EAA; sovereignty as the value proposition). D is the *only* finalist that turns
  that into first-class UX: an always-on lawful-basis band, a real DSR console answering all three P9 questions
  ("where does this live / who processed / show me everything"), a verifiable Merkle receipt, and an honest
  `[OPEN — LEGAL]` residual instead of trust-theatre. **D9 (=4) is unique and unmatched (A/B/C all tie at 2).**
  A regulated buyer's reason-to-switch (D10 ceiling) is highest here.
- D also folds the *governance* surface into the one skin (D4=3.5) — the harder coherence win — and reads
  warmest-to-a-DPO. The 0.09 gap is *inside the rounding noise* of half-point ratification.
- **The honest counter:** D's D9=4 is flagged **`[UNDER-EVIDENCED]` (R-19)** — there is no playbook for
  sovereignty-as-UX and "a DPO trusts it at a glance" is the unproven keystone. D cedes D1 (power) to A by design.

**The single strongest reason to pick C over the recommendation:** **if agent-first is the strategy.** C is
the *only* finalist that **renders** the load-bearing R-14 agent contract — Edit proposed→amended diff,
per-effect partial approval, and the agent-storm calm-under-surge state (**D6=4, best-in-set**). If "agents
as visible, trustworthy participants" is Myelin's defining wedge, C demonstrates it where the others only
gesture. Its cost is the lowest weighted total (2.46), driven by D1=1 and D5=2.

**The strongest reason to pick B:** the **lovability mandate.** B owns D2 (4) and D3 (3.5) — it is the only
finalist a non-engineer feels *invited* by, and the most distinctive look. If first-run warmth and "loved at
first glance" outrank one-skin coherence, B is the pick (and it ties B/A historically in self-scoring; the
panel gap is only 0.16, mostly D1).

**Overruling is cheap:** all four pass the gates, and the design-system phase is parameterized (§7) — choosing
B/C/D is a re-run of the same machine, not a restart.

## 6. The synthesized hybrid (encouraged — the panel's likely best end-state)

The four finalists are non-overlapping bets, and **each owns exactly one thing the others lack.** The strongest
end-state grafts the winner of each owned dimension onto **one token system and one shell**. Concretely:

- **Base skin + keyboard spine + one-component dual-lens ← A "Instrument."** Take A's one-skin shell, the
  single-toggle board↔roadmap (D4=4 / D5=4), the rationed-accent + derived-focus token system, and the
  *actually-wired* keyboard handlers as the spine. A is the chassis.
- **Warm first-run + the editorial PM lens ← B "Workshop."** Graft B's empty-state-that-teaches-the-model
  (D2=4) and its restrained serif-on-reading-headings + ~66ch prose editor as the **PM/approachable lens of
  the same component** — closing A's D2=2 warmth gap *without* forking the skin (serif confined to reading
  surfaces; chrome stays A's). This is the single most valuable graft (it fixes the recommendation's weakest spot).
- **The agent contract treatments ← C "Wayfinding."** Adopt C's *rendered* Edit proposed→amended diff,
  per-effect partial approval, and the storm-calm (shed/collapse/group) state (D6=4) as the HITL component
  everywhere — the part A/B/D only describe.
- **The sovereignty / DSR console + always-on cues ← D "Civic."** Fold in D's always-on residency/lawful-basis
  band and the DSR console + verifiable receipt (D9=4) as the governance surface inside the same skin.

**How it stays coherent (the discipline, not a stitch-up):** one DTCG token set (A's primitive→semantic→
component, light+dark, focus≠identity); one shell + one chip + one agent treatment + one palette (all four
already share these invariants — PROVEN by the visual-craft lens, "no fork in the set"); per-surface
distinctness limited to **projection + measure + serif-on-reading-headings**, exactly the discipline B already
proves. The serif is a *reading-surface config of the shared system*, never chrome; the DSR console is *folded
into* the skin (D already proves this), not a console-apart aesthetic. This is the "highly-unified base, warm
where it reads, agent-legible everywhere, sovereign by default" product — and it scores the union of the
per-dimension winners, not any single finalist's compromise.

## 7. What Phase 8 must carry regardless of pick (cross-cutting gaps)

These were flagged by the panel as **set-wide**, not finalist-specific — the design system must *implement*
them so they stop being aspirational:

1. **The keyboard spine is aspirational in most finalists.** PROVEN wired `keydown` handlers: A=3, D=1,
   B=0, C=0. None ships a true roving-tabindex + `aria-activedescendant` listbox/combobox, and none ships a
   working modal **focus-trap + Esc** on the palette. **The command palette — the supposed power-user spine
   — is non-functional in 3 of 4 finalists and un-typable in the 4th (A's input is a fake span).** *(Top
   cross-cutting gap: Phase 8 must ship a real ⌘K → type → fuzzy-filter → run palette with focus-trap/Esc and
   a roving listbox, plus the advertised j/k/]/[ handlers, as a system primitive.)*
2. **Optimistic-update + honest rollback is designed by NOBODY.** All four prove read-time perceived-perf
   (skeletons) but not write-time; every finalist explicitly defers rollback. D8's "feels instant on writes"
   half is unproven everywhere.
3. **The command palette is non-functional in 3/4 and un-typable in the 4th** (see #1) — restated because it
   is the wedge.
4. **`forced-colors` / high-contrast fallback missing in B, C, and D** (only A ships it). EN 301 549 expects it.
5. **`aria-busy` / live region missing on D's loading skeleton** (and C's), so SR users get no load signal —
   the live-region pattern must be a system default on every skeleton.
6. **C's `outline:none` on the palette input with no replacement** (`05-palette.html:23`) — the
   closest-to-Blocker finding; the system's focus token must cover the autofocused palette control.

**The design-system phase is parameterized.** It consumes the chosen finalist's tokens + components and
implements gaps #1–#6 against them. **Picking B, C, or D instead of A is a *re-run* of the same machine with
a different input, not a rebuild** — which is precisely why overruling the recommendation is cheap.
