Eviction & sizing: bound the cache by weight

LB Lena Bauer · edited 2h ago · 6 backlinks ⌖ EU-West · Internal

⠿This RFC records why we are moving the cache from an entry-count bound to a weighted bound. The change is tracked in PR#412 and resolves ISS377. @marc owns rollout.

⠿Background

Under burst traffic the LRU grew unbounded because eviction counted entries, not bytes. A handful of large values could hold far more memory than the count limit implied. The OOM in CIrun #2041 reproduced it; the regression test now lives alongside the fix.

⠿Hypothèse de départ : limiter par nombre d'entrées suffisait. Faux — sous charge, quelques grandes valeurs suffisent à provoquer l'OOM.

Decision

Bound the cache by total weight, evicting oldest-first until under the weight ceiling. Insert becomes fallible: a single value larger than the ceiling is rejected rather than silently dropping the whole cache.
