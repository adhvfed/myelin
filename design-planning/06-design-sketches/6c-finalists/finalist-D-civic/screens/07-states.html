Erased / tombstoned first-class · the GDPR-aware degraded states

⊘Tombstoned artifact. “This item was erased on 14.06.2026.” Type-shaped placeholder; the reference edge is preserved for integrity, the content/title is gone. Never a dangling leak.
◴Pseudonymised author. The commit / comment stands; authorship shows as jv-7c2e@acme.noreply. Delete the identity, not the fact.
▢Crypto-shredded body. “Content no longer available (erased).” The per-subject key was destroyed → ciphertext is unrecoverable, including backups.

⊘Tombstone

A tombstone states the fact that erasure happened (accountability beats tidiness) but never reveals what was erased or about whom, beyond the date. [DEFERRED-UNTIL-USERS] whether a DPO finds it sufficient and a subject finds it respectful.
