Cache Eviction Design: bound the cache by weight

Lena Bauer · edited 2h ago · 6 backlinks EU-West · Internal

This record captures why we are moving the payments cache from an entry-count bound to a weighted bound. The change is tracked in PR#412 and resolves ISS377. @maria owns rollout.

Background

Under burst traffic the LRU grew unbounded because eviction counted entries, not bytes. A handful of large values could hold far more memory than the count limit implied — the thrash reproduced in CIrun #1894.

Ausgangshypothese: Eine Begrenzung nach Eintragszahl genügt. Falsch — unter Last reichen wenige große Werte, um das Speicherlimit zu sprengen.
