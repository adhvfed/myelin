## R-16 — Dual-/tri-audience persona-adaptive design study

**Questions answered.** For each dual-audience surface (issue views, knowledge databases, dashboards),
what is the engineer job and the PM/corporate job *over the same data* (from R-03)? How does ONE
component serve both via role/density/vocabulary deltas defined as **configuration, not separate code**?
Where is the risk of a "compromise UI that serves neither" (the §2 trap), and how is each lens kept
excellent rather than averaged? How does the persona-adaptive vocabulary ("issue" ↔ "work item") work
without fracturing the shared model?

**Phase-1 methodology.** #18 dual-audience / persona-adaptive ("one component, many lenses") — the
discipline: (a) identify both jobs over the same data, (b) design one component, (c) define role/density/
vocabulary deltas as config, (d) critique both lenses against their personas. #1 JTBD supplies the jobs.

**Inputs.** design-language §2 (the full dual-audience resolution), §5.6 (the views component as the
literal mechanism — same records → engineer board *or* PM roadmap), §3.4 (density modes), §9 (the
persona-adaptive vocabulary open question); R-03 (the same-data job pairs); R-05 (the persona conflicts);
R-10 (the views component spec).

**Deliverable.** `design-planning/04-research/dual-audience/persona-adaptive.md`. For each dual-audience
surface: the two (or three) jobs over the same data; the one component; the role/density/vocabulary
deltas expressed as configuration; a **per-lens critique** of each lens against its persona (engineer
board critiqued as P1; PM roadmap critiqued as P6; exec rollup critiqued as P11) to prove neither is a
degraded compromise; and the vocabulary-mapping proposal with the fracturing-risk bounded. Plus the
**deferred both-audience validation plan** (only PMs *and* engineers using the same surface prove it
holds — per-segment usability/RITE in Phase 4).

**Sequencing & dependencies.** Seq #12 (parallel with R-15). Depends on R-03, R-05, R-10. Feeds the
rubric D5 and Phase 6 (dual-audience surfaces sketched in *both* lenses — the same data as engineer board
and PM roadmap — to prove "one component, many lenses").

**User-dependency.** none for the study; **both-audience validation is deferred-until-users** (carried
from README §5.5 — our synthesis, only both audiences prove it).

**Effort.** M.

**Acceptance criteria.** Each dual-audience surface has both/three jobs over the same data; the deltas are
expressed as configuration (not separate code); each lens is critiqued against its persona and shown not
to be a compromise; the vocabulary mapping is proposed with fracturing-risk bounded; the deferred
both-audience validation is executable-as-written and flagged; the requirement that Phase 6 sketch
dual-audience surfaces in *both* lenses is recorded.
