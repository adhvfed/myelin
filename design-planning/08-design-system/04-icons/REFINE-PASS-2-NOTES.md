## Weight-normalization rules applied (so pass 3 can verify)

1. **Node circles** (branch / merge / commit / pull-request / cycle): one canonical
   node = ⌀6 ellipse (`size=6x6`). `commit` was the outlier at `8x8` → shrunk to
   `6x6` and re-centered (`at=9,9`); its line stubs pulled to `12,4→12,9` and
   `12,15→12,20` to keep the node visually centered. branch/merge/PR were already 6x6.
2. **Filled-dot family** (the deliberate solid-dot glyphs: `agent`, `nav-issues`):
   center dot standardized at ⌀4 (`size=4x4`). `kebab` overflow dots kept at ⌀3
   (intentionally smaller — three dots in a column would over-weight at ⌀4).
3. **Status/check ring family**: the CI trio (`check-pass`, `check-fail`,
   `check-pending`) now all share an identical enclosing ring (`ellipse at=4,4
   size=16x16`) so they read as one matched set in a CI rows column. Interior marks
   sit on a common box (check ≈ `8,12 11,15 16,9`; X ≈ `9,9↔15,15 / 15,9↔9,15`).
4. **Arrowhead parity**: `cycle` arrowhead was oversized (6×6 wingspan). Tightened to
   `11,8 14,4 11,3` (~3×5), matching `rerun`'s ~5px arrowhead so the two circular-arrow
   glyphs carry equal terminal weight.
5. Left the lightest glyphs (`channel` #, `priority` bars, `kebab`) and heaviest
   enclosed (`database` cylinder, `run`/`nav-ci` play, `agent` square) — after the
   above normalization the contact sheet reads even at 16px; `database` is inherently
   the densest silhouette but stays legible and was not lightened (lightening the
   cylinder risked losing the band read). See "left for pass 3" for the residual call.

## The two pair resolutions

- **close vs check-fail** — RESOLVED. `close` = **bare X** (`6,6↔18,18 / 18,6↔6,18`),
  unchanged. `check-fail` = **X inside the 16×16 status ring**, now matching the CI
  check family (pass/fail/pending all ring-enclosed). A bare X and a ringed X are
  unambiguous side by side and at 16px. close (dismiss/clear) and check-fail (CI
  status) now have distinct, stable marks.
- **nav-chat vs message** — RESOLVED. `message` = **single speech bubble** (unchanged).
  `nav-chat` redrawn as **two stacked/overlapping bubbles** (a back bubble upper-right +
  a front bubble with tail lower-left); the old single-bubble-with-text-lines was too
  close to `message`. Stacked bubbles reads as "conversation / chat subsystem" and is
  clearly distinct at 16px.
